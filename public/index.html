    What happens after you paste the link.

    1. Download

      The video is fetched at up to 720p on this server.

    2. Read the captions

      YouTube's captions become a transcript with timestamps.

    3. Pick five moments

      Each stretch of speech is scored. The five best that don't overlap win.

    4. Cut and reframe

      Each moment is trimmed, cropped to your format, and captioned.

    What to expect, honestly.

    This is a simple clipper, not a studio.

    • Cropping is centered

      Clips are cropped from the middle of the frame. There is no face tracking, so a speaker off to one side can be cut off.

    • Picks are rule-based

      Moments are chosen by scoring speech, not by understanding the video. Review them before you post.

    • Captions need captions

      On-screen text appears only when the video has captions. Without them, clips are spaced evenly through the video.

    • It takes a few minutes

      Long videos take longer. Clips are deleted from the server after a couple of hours, so download what you want.

    Questions people ask first.

    Which videos can I use?

    Only videos you own or have permission to clip. Downloading other people's videos can break YouTube's terms and copyright law. Private videos, members-only videos, and live streams do not work.

    Why did my link fail?

    YouTube sometimes blocks downloads that come from hosting providers and asks the visitor to prove they are not a bot. If you see that message, the site owner needs to configure a cookies file for the server.

    Why are there no captions on my clips?

    Either the video has no captions, or the server could not render text onto the video. The page tells you which when it happens.

    How long can the video be?

    The limit is set by the site owner. Long videos use more time and disk space.
